# Third-Party Notices

## Best-Flutter-UI-Templates

The mobile layout of this app — the card-led dashboard, the soft elevation, and
the bottom bar with a raised centre action — is adapted from the design of
[Best-Flutter-UI-Templates](https://github.com/mitesh77/Best-Flutter-UI-Templates)
by Mitesh Chodvadiya, chiefly its `fitness_app` and `hotel_booking` templates.

No source from that project is included here. The templates are written in Dart
for Flutter; this app is React and Tailwind, so the layouts were reimplemented
rather than copied. The credit is recorded because the design lineage is real.
